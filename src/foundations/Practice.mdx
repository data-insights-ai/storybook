# How the brand behaves in a screen.

## Motifs

Use a form for the meaning it carries.

- Brackets mark a temporal interval. They are not a decorative frame.
- A graph is a few nodes and edges, about seven at most, for an evidence chain.
- The truth-tittle is one gold dot per view. It is not repeated beside itself.
- A timestamp is ISO-8601, `YYYY-MM-DD`, in IBM Plex Mono. Small gold type is Gold 700 (`#7C5F17`). Gold 600 does not clear AA at that size.
- The section mark `§` is for a numbered legal clause.
- A hairline is 1px at 12% ink, and it divides. It is not decoration.

## Charts

A chart is an argument. Show the axis. Name the source. Mark an estimate. Navy carries the series. Gold is the one value the eye should land on. A dashed tail is an estimate. Other series, when we are the story, are grey. A range is a band. The solid line is the median. Every row that states a number carries its source, and an estimate is labelled `est.`

## Icons

Icons label an action. They are geometric, on a 24×24 grid, with a 1.5px stroke, round caps, and a 2px corner. The stroke is navy on a warm surface. Gold is only the active or cited state. Lucide is the set we use, at that weight.

## Motion

Move something because it changed: a value, a loaded source, a cited claim. If a cut will do, use a cut.

Transitions sit between 120ms and 240ms, on `--di-ease`, which is an ease-out. No spring, no overshoot, no bounce. One thing moves at a time. A bracket arrives in a frame or two. It does not glide in.
