# Write like an analyst.

Short sentences. Specific nouns. Verbs that promise only what can be proved. When the sentence reaches for emphasis, it cites.

We are precise, evidence-first, and quiet about confidence. We are comfortable with “we don’t know”. We are not hype-forward, chatty, cute, or emoji-heavy. We do not make a guarantee we cannot audit.

## The headline test

If we would not say it in a regulator’s waiting room, we do not say it in a headline.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Pass | AI that cites its sources. Specific, and defensible. |
| Fail | Revolutionary intelligence for the modern enterprise. Two hype words, and no noun a regulator could audit. |
| Pass | Every answer is replayable back to the data it came from. The promise can be shown. |

## Prefer, and avoid

Prefer grounded, cited, traceable, provable, evidence, replayable, temporal, auditable, verified.

Avoid intelligent, revolutionary, unlock, leverage, seamlessly, eliminated, game-changing, supercharge, and emoji.

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Revolutionary AI that unlocks insights at scale. | A temporal knowledge graph. Every answer cites its source. |
| Seamlessly eliminate hallucinations with our intelligent engine. | When we cannot cite a source, we say so. No source, no answer. |
| Empower your team to leverage game-changing data. | Analysts replay any answer back to the rows that produced it. |
