  title="Read first, change on purpose."
  lede="A console page has one job and shows it first. A list page finds and opens records, a detail page states one record and lets a reader change a part of it, a create flow makes a new one. Overview, editing and destruction never share one undifferentiated column."
>
  <Section
    eyebrow="Before the layout"
    title="Who comes here, and what for."
    lede="Write down, per page, who arrives, their three most frequent tasks, what they must see first, and where they expect each action. Place by that: nothing frequent below the fold, nothing destructive next to something routine."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>The primary action of the page — `New …`, `Invite people`, `Open product` — sits in `PageHeaderAside`, top right.</Rule>
      <Rule>Actions on the whole list — export, a view switch — sit in the list toolbar's `ToolbarEnd` (`Toolbar role="search"`), and apply the current search and filters.</Rule>
      <Rule>Actions on one row sit in the row: frequent safe ones in a `Toolbar variant="row"` shown on hover, all of them in the row's `Menu`. Opening the row is the row itself.</Rule>
      <Rule>Changing a part of a record is the `Edit` of its `DetailSection`. Destroying it is a `DangerAction` in the `Panel tone="danger"`, last on the page, through a `ConfirmDialog`.</Rule>
      <Rule>Frequent actions get a key: `/` search, `n` new, `e` edit or export (`useShortcut`), shown beside the control.</Rule>
    </Rules>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="Affordance and space"
    title="Underline means clickable."
    lede="A reader should never have to try whether something opens. What can be clicked looks clickable in one way, and nothing else does."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>Only a link, a button or a disclosure is underlined. No decorative underline, no underlined name that opens nothing, no dotted definition underline: a term with a definition shows it on hover and focus with the help cursor.</Rule>
      <Rule>A link is the text colour with a cornflower underline (`TextLink`), in prose and at a block's edge alike. It darkens on hover and carries the focus ring.</Rule>
      <Rule>A name that opens a record is a link, or the whole row is the target, with a hover tint and the pointer. Never an underlined name in a row that does not open.</Rule>
      <Rule>Never a surface on the same surface. A panel or tile inside a panel takes the inset tone and drops its edge; a table on a surface drops its frame.</Rule>
      <Rule>Text and controls keep at least 12px from the inner edge of the surface that holds them. Table rows, menu rows and media run to the edge on purpose.</Rule>
      <Rule>One outline per object. No outlined element sits inside another outlined element: a selection inside a frame is a fill, and items in a framed row are either framed cells with no track, or unframed inside one frame. A focus ring is the exception, because it is temporary.</Rule>
      <Rule>A ring shows only on keyboard focus. After a pointer click there is none.</Rule>
      <Rule>One label style per block: mono capitals above values, or sentence-case labels beside them, never both side by side.</Rule>
      <Rule>An opened part belongs to its head: the same surface, the same inset, the text on the title's edge, on a phone too.</Rule>
      <Rule>Footers and secondary zones (a table footer, the hints of a command bar) are a quieter tone than the content. Controls under a table or a list start on its text edge, or sit centred as one unit.</Rule>
      <Rule>The story tests enforce the surface, clearance, underline and outline rules, in both themes, both densities and at both widths (`.storybook/tests/surfaces`).</Rule>
    </Rules>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="List page"
    title="Find, filter, open."
    lede="A list page: the header with the primary action, a sticky search toolbar, then the table, or one empty notice in its place."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>`Page width="list"`: `PageHeader` with the primary action in `PageHeaderAside`, a sticky `Toolbar role="search"`, then an `InfiniteTable` — or a `Notice variant="empty"` in its place.</Rule>
      <Rule>Search and filters run on the server and live in the URL, so a link, reload and back show the same list.</Rule>
      <Rule>The list loads page by page with a cursor and loads the next page before the end. No pagination control, no `Load more` button, no client-side filter over a partial list.</Rule>
      <Rule>The toolbar and the table head stay in view while the list scrolls.</Rule>
      <Rule>Entries pushed in realtime appear in place in a sorted list; in a newest-first list they wait behind the `n new` notice while the reader is further down.</Rule>
      <Rule>The first load is a skeleton, not a spinner. An empty result names why and offers one way out: clear the filters, or create the first entry.</Rule>
      <Rule>A row's detail and a create or edit form open in a side `Dialog`. Where the page has its side column an `md` dialog docks there and the list keeps working beside it; narrower, and always at `lg` and `xl`, the dialog covers the page. A confirmation stays a centred `Dialog` or `ConfirmDialog`.</Rule>
    </Rules>
    <Columns>
      <Lines title="Do" items={["The create form opens in a side Dialog or its own page from the header action.", "Filters are selects whose first option names them: All roles.", "One empty Notice, no eyebrow, an icon and the one action."]} />
      <Lines title="Don't" items={["An add form that is always open above the list.", "A list that loads everything and filters in the browser.", "An empty card repeating the page's eyebrow over the full width."]} />
    </Columns>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="Detail page"
    title="One record: summary, sections, danger last."
    lede="A detail page: the header with the way back to the list, the record's status and key facts, then its sections, and the danger zone last."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>`Page width="detail"`: a `PageHeader` with `PageHeaderCrumbs` back to the list, the status beside the name and key facts under the lede, then `DetailSection`s, then a `Panel tone="danger"` holding the `DangerAction`s.</Rule>
      <Rule>A section reads by default — a `FactList layout="columns"`, ids as `CopyValue`. Its `Edit` switches only that section into its form with Save and Cancel; focus moves in and back.</Rule>
      <Rule>A change of several independent settings in one section (switches) collects in a `Toolbar sticky="bottom"` (unsaved changes) instead of saving each.</Rule>
      <Rule>A change too large for a section opens a side `Dialog`. A record opened from a list row may live in a side `Dialog` too, with the same sections inside.</Rule>
      <Rule>Each `DangerAction` states the consequence and has one danger button. When it is unavailable, `note` says why — "You are the only owner" — and the button is disabled, not hidden. A disabled danger button keeps its red edge in both themes, so it still reads as what it would do.</Rule>
      <Rule>An irreversible action asks for the record's name in the `ConfirmDialog` (`confirmText`), and the danger is stated inside the dialog itself, not only on the page behind it. What follows is a `Toast` receipt with the audit id, never an Undo.</Rule>
    </Rules>
    <Columns>
      <Lines title="Do" items={["Facts as a FactList; Edit beside the section title.", "Leave and Delete in the danger panel at the end.", "The disabled Leave says what to do first."]} />
      <Lines title="Don't" items={["A Name field with a Rename button under the read-only facts.", "Leave as an outlined button at the bottom right like any other.", "A destructive button inside a table row."]} />
    </Columns>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="Create flow"
    title="Choose, fill in, review, create."
    lede="Choose the type, fill in the details, review what will be created. On its own page, or in a side dialog when it is short."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>`CreateFlow` with its `Steps`: the type as `ChoiceGroup appearance="cards"`, then the details, then the review. On its own page `Page width="detail"` with `CreateFlow width="page"`, or in a side `Dialog`.</Rule>
      <Rule>Enter moves on, Back keeps what was entered, Next stays disabled until the step is complete.</Rule>
      <Rule>On a page the steps run in one line from the content's left edge, each name beside its number, and the step is a panel like a record's section: its title, one lede, its body, the actions as its footer. The type's cards, a grid or a preview beside the fields use the panel's width; a lone field, a switch and a list of terms keep the form measure.</Rule>
      <Rule>After creating, land on the new record's detail page, with a toast.</Rule>
    </Rules>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="Proportions"
    title="Width by content, rhythm by token."
    lede="A page is as wide as what it holds. Spacing between blocks belongs to the page, never to the block."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>Width: a list (`list`), a record or settings (`detail`) share one frame: from a page width of 1280px the content takes all but a side column at `--di-rail-width` (320px) on the right, which holds the record's `PageAside` or the list's open row and otherwise stays empty; narrower the content takes the whole width. An overview or dashboard is `full`, the whole frame. A create flow on its own page is `detail` too, its step a panel in the content; `form`, `--di-measure-form` (720px), is a single column of fields. Prose stays at `--di-measure-prose` (62ch).</Rule>
      <Rule>Rhythm: header to content `--di-rhythm-header` (24), toolbar to its list `--di-rhythm-toolbar` (12), section to section `--di-rhythm-section` (32), field to field `--di-rhythm-field` (16). `Page` applies them.</Rule>
      <Rule>Fields by content: text, an email or a URL spans the form column; a select is as wide as its options; a number is short (`Stack variant="form"`). On one line, the first field grows and the button sits on the control row (`Toolbar fillFirst`).</Rule>
      <Rule>Headings: one `h1` per page. Section titles only where there are several sections. No eyebrow repeated inside cards, empty states or dialogs on a page that already has one.</Rule>
      <Rule>The frame: a `Sidebar`, then in `SidebarInset` an `AppShellBar` and the `AppShellContent`. The bar is one row at every width: the trigger, the page's name, and an `AppShellBarEnd` whose status and refresh fold into one "More" button on a phone. What sits in `AppShellBarKeep` stays — the notification bell, because its count is its point.</Rule>
      <Rule>At phone width the sidebar is a drawer from the inline start, the gutter is 16px, toolbars wrap, the search takes its own line, a side `Dialog` takes the screen and nothing scrolls sideways but a table frame.</Rule>
    </Rules>
  </Section>

  <Section
    eyebrow="Feedback"
    title="Say what happened, where it happened."
    lede="A save confirms with a toast; an error stays next to the control that failed. Optimistic updates only where a failure can be undone visibly."
  >
    <Rules>
      <Rule>Every visible string is a prop. A product passes its own language — the components ship none.</Rule>
      <Rule>A `Dialog` is a native dialog: focus moves in, is trapped, and returns to the opener. Every icon-only button has a name.</Rule>
    </Rules>
  </Section>
