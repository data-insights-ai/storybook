# The mark, then the name.

The Logo story shows the lockups. These are the rules that go with them.

The brackets are a temporal interval, `[t0, t1]`, the window in which a claim holds. The peak is an A, and the simplest graph. The stem is the I. The nodes are the feet of the graph. One of them is gold. That tittle is verified truth, and there is only one of them in a view.

## Which lockup

The primary lockup is the default, on ivory or paper. Inverse is for navy 900 or navy 950, never for gold and never for a photograph. Monochrome is for single-ink print, embossing, and merchandise; the gold tittle becomes navy. The mark alone is for a favicon, an avatar, or an app tile, and the name is set nearby.

## Clear space and size

X is the cap height of the mark. Keep at least 1X of empty space on all four sides.

The wordmark is at least 20px tall on screen and 10mm tall in print. The mark alone is at least 16px, or 6mm in print.

## What we do not do

Scale the logo uniformly. Leave its colours as navy and gold, or the inverse pair. Keep it horizontal, flat, and on a solid ivory or navy field.

Do not stretch it, skew it, recolour it, rotate it, or add a shadow. Do not place it on gold, or on a busy photograph.

## Next to another mark

Give both marks equal optical height, not the same bounding box. Separate them with a vertical rule, and leave one cap-height of clear space on either side of that rule. They share a baseline. In a strip of three or more, use the monochrome logo. Leave the other brand in its own colours. A photograph is not a background for the pair.
