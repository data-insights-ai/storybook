    <Rules>
      <Rule>A free-standing control (a button, a field, a link at a block's edge) takes the ring outside, at the offset, with the ground showing between.</Rule>
      <Rule>An item that touches its neighbours or sits in a clipping frame takes the ring inside (`outline-offset: var(--di-focus-ring-inset)`): menu and option rows, sidebar items, table rows and a table's scroll frame, tabs and the tab panel, pages, segments, a field's joined steps, a dialog's close.</Rule>
      <Rule>A framed item in a group (a choice card, a toggle, a tag) takes the ring on its edge (`outline-offset: 0`). Chosen, its cornflower edge and the ring read as one thicker edge: focus and selection are one indicator, never two rings.</Rule>
      <Rule>Inside a strong fill (the current page) the ring takes --di-focus-ring-on-strong, so it holds 3:1 against the fill in both themes. A region re-colours the ring through --di-focus-ring-color, never through outline-color.</Rule>
      <Rule>Every scroll frame keeps a ring's reach (--di-focus-ring-reach) as scroll padding; one under a sticky bar or above sticky actions keeps their height too. A clip that must stay reaches a ring's width past its content.</Rule>
      <Rule>A link in running text hugs its words (`outline-offset: 0`), so the ring stays off the lines above and below.</Rule>
      <Rule>The story tests Tab through every story, in both themes, both densities and at both widths, and fail a ring that is clipped, overlaps another control or item, falls under 3:1, doubles a selection edge or lies under a sticky layer (`.storybook/tests/focus`). `pnpm lint:tokens` keeps every outline on these tokens.</Rule>
    </Rules>
