# datAInsights

Component workbench for the datAInsights brand. Foundations are the brand reference for building: colour, type, the mark, voice, and motion. The screens are composed examples of the console frame. They do not belong to one product.

## Tokens

Three layers.

- **Primitive** — the brand-book values, plus a separate status ramp for operational state. No component names a primitive colour, typeface, or radius.
- **Semantic** — what a thing is for: page, surface, text, fill, focus, sidebar, status. Light is the console as drawn. Dark remaps the same roles.
- **Component** — slots such as `--di-button-primary-bg`. One control can be restyled without touching the others.

Warm paper (`#F4F1E8`) is the ground; navy (`#0A1F44`) is the ink and, in daylight, the only thing that fills a control. There is no invented accent, so what writes is what commits. After dark the fill turns gold, because navy cannot act against a navy ground — that is the single exception.

Gold is the scarce mark and it carries meaning, not emphasis. A **filled dot** means the value is recorded and verifiable. A **hollow ring** means a model inferred it and nothing has vouched for it yet. Status (verified, anomaly, critical, recorded) has its own washes so a screen full of rows never spends the gold.

Small gold text on paper uses Seal ink (`#7A5C0E`); the seal colour itself (`#C9A24A`) is a mark, never type. On navy, gold type uses Gold 400.

## Type

Space Grotesk is everything a person reads as prose. JetBrains Mono is everything a machine wrote: identifiers, hashes, timestamps, measured values, and the § marks that number a screen. Which track a string sits in is a statement about where it came from, not a texture. Figures are tabular, so a column of readings does not jitter as it updates.

## Controls

Buttons, fields, tables, and navigation are native elements. A headless widget library is worth adding when a control needs it — a combobox, a menu, a dialog — and not before.

Sample copy is English. Strings are props, so a product can pass another language. Icons and other elements are slots, not controls.

Sample people are placeholders. Their addresses are `@data-insights.ai`.
